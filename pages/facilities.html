State-of-the-Art Facilities

Explore our modern infrastructure designed for holistic development

Our Commitment to Excellence

At PM SHRI Government Senior Secondary School, Mandal, Bhilwara, we believe that world-class facilities are essential for nurturing young minds. Our campus combines modern infrastructure with eco-friendly practices to create an optimal learning environment.

Modern Infrastructure

Recently upgraded facilities with state-of-the-art equipment and technology integration across all departments.

Student-Centric Design

Spacious classrooms, well-equipped laboratories, and recreational areas designed with student comfort and safety in mind.

Eco-Friendly Campus

Solar power initiatives, rainwater harvesting, and green spaces promoting environmental consciousness.

Academic Infrastructure

Our academic facilities are designed to foster curiosity, innovation, and excellence in learning.

Smart Classrooms

All classrooms are equipped with interactive whiteboards, projectors, and audio-visual systems for engaging and interactive learning experiences.

Technology integration helps visualize complex concepts and makes learning more effective and enjoyable.

Science Laboratories

Separate, well-equipped laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, and Biology with modern apparatus, safety equipment, and individual workstations for hands-on experimentation.

Regular updates ensure students work with current technology and industry-standard equipment.

Computer & IT Center

Advanced computer lab with latest hardware, licensed software, and high-speed internet connectivity.

Courses include basic computer literacy, programming, web design, and digital literacy skills essential for the modern world.

Library & Resource Center

A spacious, well-stocked library with extensive collection of books, journals, reference materials, and digital resources across all subjects and genres.

Features include reading rooms, reference section, periodicals corner, and digital access to educational resources.

Language Lab

Advanced facility for enhancing language skills through interactive software, audio-visual aids, and pronunciation practice.
