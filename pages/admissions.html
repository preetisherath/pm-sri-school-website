Eligibility Criteria

Find the specific requirements for different admission categories below:

For Class 11 (Higher Secondary)

Academic Requirement

Minimum 60% aggregate in Class 10 board examinations

Age Limit

15-18 years as of July 1st of the admission year

Documents Required

  • Class 10 mark sheet (original and photocopy)
  • Birth certificate
  • Transfer certificate from previous school
  • Character certificate
  • Recent passport-sized photographs
  • Aadhaar card copy

Stream Selection

Students can choose from Science, Commerce, or Arts stream based on their interests and Class 10 performance

For Class 9 (Secondary)

Academic Requirement

Minimum 55% aggregate in Class 8 examinations

Age Limit

13-16 years as of July 1st of the admission year

Documents Required

  • Class 8 mark sheet (original and photocopy)
  • Birth certificate
  • Transfer certificate from previous school
  • Character certificate
  • Recent passport-sized photographs
  • Aadhaar card copy

For Transfer Students

Academic Requirement

Passing marks in previous class

Availability

Subject to vacancy in respective class and stream

Documents Required

  • Mark sheet of last qualifying examination
  • Transfer certificate from previous school
  • Character certificate
  • Birth certificate
  • Recent passport-sized photographs
  • Aadhaar card copy

Note

Admission subject to compatibility of syllabus and availability of seats

How to Apply

  1. Step 1: Obtain Application Form

    Download from our website or collect from school office between 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM on working days

  2. Step 2: Fill Application Form

    Complete all sections accurately and attach required documents

  3. Step 3: Submit Application

    Submit the completed form along with documents to the school office before the deadline

  4. Step 4: Entrance Test & Interaction

    Appear for the scheduled entrance test followed by student-parent interaction session

  5. Step 5: Result & Admission

    Check results on the announced date and complete admission formalities if selected
