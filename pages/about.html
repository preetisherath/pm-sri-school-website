About Our School

Discover our commitment to educational excellence and holistic development

PM SHRI Government Senior Secondary School, Mandal, Bhilwara stands as a premier institution dedicated to nurturing young minds, fostering intellectual curiosity, and shaping future leaders through a balanced approach to academics, sports, arts, and community service.

Our Vision

To empower every student with knowledge, skills, and values to become responsible global citizens and leaders of tomorrow.

Our Mission

To provide quality education that fosters intellectual growth, creativity, and character development in a nurturing and inclusive environment.

Our team of highly qualified and experienced educators brings passion and expertise to every classroom. With regular professional development programs and a student-centric approach, our teachers mentor learners to achieve their full potential.
