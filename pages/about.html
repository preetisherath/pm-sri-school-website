About Our School

Discover our commitment to educational excellence and holistic development

Our Vision

To empower every student with knowledge, skills, and values to become responsible global citizens and leaders of tomorrow.

Our Mission

To provide quality education that fosters intellectual growth, creativity, and character development in a nurturing and inclusive environment.

Our History

Established with the vision of providing exceptional education to the youth of Mandal and surrounding areas, PM SHRI Government Senior Secondary School, Mandal, Bhilwara has been a beacon of academic excellence since its inception.

Core Values

  • Excellence: Striving for the highest standards in all endeavors
  • Integrity: Upholding honesty, ethics, and moral courage
  • Respect: Valuing diversity and treating everyone with dignity
  • Innovation: Embracing creativity and progressive thinking
  • Service: Committing to contribute positively to society
