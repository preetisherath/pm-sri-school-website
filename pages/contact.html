Frequently Asked Questions

What are the admission procedures?

Admission procedures vary by grade level. Please visit our admissions page or contact our admissions office at +91 9352599991 for detailed information about eligibility criteria, required documents, and important dates.

What streams are offered at the senior secondary level?

We offer three streams: Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Mathematics), Commerce (Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics), and Arts/Humanities (History, Geography, Political Science, Sociology, Psychology, Economics).

What are the school timings?

Regular school hours are from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM for all grades. Office hours for inquiries are 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM Monday through Friday, and 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM on Saturdays.

Does the school provide transportation facilities?

Yes, we provide bus services covering various routes in Mandal and surrounding areas. For route information, timings, and fees, please contact the school office.
