Academic Excellence

Explore our comprehensive curriculum designed for holistic development

At PM SHRI Government Senior Secondary School, Mandal, Bhilwara, we believe education is the foundation for building bright futures. Our academic programs blend rigorous scholastic standards with innovative teaching methodologies, ensuring every student develops the knowledge, skills, and character needed to excel in higher education and beyond.

Our Academic Vision

To foster intellectual curiosity, critical thinking, and a lifelong love for learning in every student through student-centered, technology-enhanced education.

Our Academic Mission

To provide a nurturing and challenging environment where students achieve academic excellence while developing essential life skills, creativity, and social responsibility.

Streams Offered

We offer three main streams for students to choose based on their interests and career aspirations:

Science Stream

Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Mathematics, English, Hindi/Sanskrit

Ideal For: Students aspiring for careers in Medicine, Engineering, Research, Engineering, Research, and Technology

Career Paths: Doctor, Engineer, Scientist, Pharmacist, Biotechnologist, Data Analyst

Commerce Stream

Subjects: Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, Mathematics, English, Hindi/Sanskrit

Ideal For: Students interested in Business, Finance, Economics, and Management

Career Paths: Chartered Accountant, Business Analyst, Financial Advisor, Entrepreneur, Banker, Marketing Manager

Arts/Humanities Stream

Subjects: History, Geography, Political Science, Sociology, Psychology, Economics, English, Hindi/Sanskrit

Ideal For: Students passionate about Social Sciences, Languages, Literature, and Humanities

Career Paths: Lawyer, Journalist, Teacher, Psychologist, Social Worker, Civil Services, Content Creator

Curriculum Highlights

Well-Structured Syllabus

Our curriculum follows the prescribed board guidelines while incorporating modern pedagogical approaches:

  • Conceptual Understanding: Focus on grasping fundamentals rather than rote learning
  • Practical Applications: Laboratory work, projects, and field visits
  • Interdisciplinary Learning: Connections between subjects for holistic understanding
  • Skill Development: Emphasis on analytical, communication, and interpersonal skills
  • Value Education: Integration of ethics, environmental awareness, and civic responsibility

Assessment & Evaluation

Our evaluation system is designed to provide comprehensive feedback on student progress:

  • Formative Assessments: Regular quizzes, class tests, assignments, and project work
  • Summative Assessments: Terminal exams and annual examinations
  • Practical Exams: Laboratory work and project evaluations
  • Continuous Assessment: Based on participation, presentations, and consistent performance
  • Parent-Teacher Collaboration: Regular updates on student progress through meetings and reports

Teaching Methodology

Our experienced faculty employs diverse teaching strategies to cater to different learning styles:

  • Interactive Lectures: Use of multimedia, discussions, and real-world examples
  • Project-Based Learning: Hands-on projects that encourage problem-solving and teamwork
  • Flipped Classroom: Pre-class materials followed by in-depth classroom activities
  • Technology Integration: Smart classrooms, educational apps, and online resources
  • Differentiated Instruction: Tailored approaches to meet individual student needs
